Welcome to Vijayawada

Vijayawada is a beautiful city in Andhra Pradesh, India. It is famous for its temples, historical places, Krishna River, and delicious traditional food.

Places to Visit

  • Kanaka Durga Temple
  • Prakasam Barrage
  • Undavalli Caves
  • Bhavani Island
  • Gandhi Hill

Things to Do

  • Visit famous temples
  • Enjoy a boat ride
  • Explore historical monuments
  • Enjoy beautiful river views

Food to Try

  • Andhra Chicken Curry
  • Gongura Pachadi
  • Punugulu
  • Andhra Meals
